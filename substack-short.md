# Turn any EPUB into an audiobook, free

Some books never get recorded. This makes one — entirely on your own machine.

- **Free and private.** Local Kokoro TTS. No API keys, no per-book cost, nothing uploaded.
- **Apple Books ready.** A single chaptered `.m4b` with cover art.
- **Resumable.** A 5-hour book takes ~6 hours to render; stop and continue any time, down to the chunk.
- **Web UI or terminal.** Drop in a file, pick a narrator, press one button.
